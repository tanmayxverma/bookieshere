YPT is Bookie’s built-in study timer. Start it when you sit down to study and stop it when you finish; Bookie totals your daily hours, maintains your streak and ranks you on a live leaderboard with other aspirants — so consistency becomes a game you want to win.

Does Bookie host the books and test papers itself?

Bookie is an index and organiser: it links to study resources and provides the tools around them — timer, leaderboards, community and mentor. Rights holders who want a link removed can contact us any time via the Support or DMCA page.
