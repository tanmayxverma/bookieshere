Bookie — Free JEE Study Material, Mock Tests & Study Timer

Bookie is a free study cockpit built by a JEE aspirant, for JEE aspirants. It brings your entire JEE Main and JEE Advanced preparation into one place: a library of study material, test series from India’s top coaching institutes, a study timer with live leaderboards, a community where you can ask doubts, and an AI study mentor for quick help. There are no ads and no paywall — every core feature is free.

Study material and test series in one library

The Bookie library collects the books, coaching modules, notes and previous-year questions that aspirants actually use, organised by subject — Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics — and by type, so you can find the right resource in seconds instead of hunting through group chats. Alongside the material, the Tests hub links test series and mock tests from institutes like Allen, Resonance, Vedantu and Motion, including All-India test series, so you can practise on the same papers as aspirants across the country.

How Bookie works

  1. Sign in free with Google, email, or continue as a guest — no payment and no card required.

Frequently asked questions

Is Bookie really free?

Yes. The study material library, test series links, study timer, community and AI mentor are all free to use, with no ads. Bookie is a solo project run alongside the owner’s own JEE preparation; readers who find it useful can optionally support it with a small donation on the Buy Me a Chai page.

Who is Bookie for?

Bookie is made for students preparing for JEE Main and JEE Advanced — Class 11, Class 12 and drop-year aspirants who want their books, mock tests, study tracking and doubt-solving in one place instead of scattered across apps and Telegram groups.

What data does Bookie collect when I sign in?

Signing in with Google gives us only your name, email address and profile picture, used to create and manage your account. We never sell user data. The full details are in our Privacy Policy, and you can use the main features as a guest without signing in at all.

Does Bookie host the books and test papers itself?

Bookie is an index and organiser: it links to study resources shared for aspirants and provides the tools around them — the timer, leaderboards, community and mentor. If you are a rights holder and want a link removed, contact us through the Support page or see the DMCA page.
